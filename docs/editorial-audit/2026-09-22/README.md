## Business and service model

myCHEF offers coordinated culinary services in Dubai through its chef and culinary partner network. The website contains three distinct buying journeys:

1. **Recurring household cooking.** The household chooses frequency and visit format. The service covers matching, scheduling, preferences and ongoing coordination. Pricing is generated by the household pricing model; it is not an event per-person rate.
2. **One-off catering and dining.** The host supplies the occasion, venue, date, guest count and menu direction. Drop-off food, staffed buffets, live cooking, plated dining and yacht service have different inclusions and minimums.
3. **Corporate and institutional services.** Office meals, events, nurseries, schools, hospitals and managed canteens require a service brief, kitchen/site assessment and written responsibilities. Institutional and medical requirements cannot be inferred from ordinary private-party menus.

The main conversion channels are WhatsApp and the enquiry form. Calculator choices now travel into the enquiry; any indicative price is recalculated from the current pricing engine. A 15-minute target means an initial response during 9am–11pm Dubai time. A tailored proposal follows review of requirements and availability.

## Website and content architecture

The application is React 19 with React Router, TypeScript and Vite. Tailwind supplies styles; GSAP controls many entrances and scroll animations. Vercel serves the generated HTML and SPA fallback, with redirects defined in `vercel.json`. GitHub `main` is the deployment source.

| Responsibility | Source and maintenance rule |
| --- | --- |
| Routes | `src/routes.tsx`; dynamic location, topic and comparison paths also need inventory coverage |
| Page-specific copy | `src/pages/` and imported `src/content/` modules |
| Homepage | `src/sections/` plus `src/pages/Home.tsx` |
| Locations | `src/data/locations.ts`; many routes intentionally remain noindex |
| Handoff guides | 16 route-mapped JSON articles in `src/content/seo-pages/`; rendered by `HandoffPage` |
| Comparison articles | `blog/data/*.json`; generated with `scripts/ryze-convert.mjs` |
| Metadata | Page props plus `src/content/seoAuditOverrides.ts`; the override can take precedence |
| Keyword ownership | `docs/seo/myCHEF-AE-SEO-STANDARD.json`; generated locks are projections |
| Breadcrumbs and related links | Generated maps plus some manual modules; all must respect parked/redirect status |
| Prices | Separate household, catering and yacht modules; do not merge their units |
| Enquiries | Inquiry/form components, shared brief helpers, WhatsApp and API/email paths |
| Tracking | SPA analytics, Vercel Analytics/Speed Insights and Microsoft Clarity and existing WebGrower/Ryze integrations |

The commercial SEO JSON collection also contains legacy body copy that is no longer rendered. Editing those unused bodies would not rewrite the website. Source-file dependencies in each page record identify the implementation that matters.

## Editorial changes

The copy now explains the service in direct, considered British English. The first two logged passes contain 685 exact text changes, followed by complete article rewrites, metadata reconciliation, image-caption revisions and consistency fixes.

- Clarified recurring chefs, one-off dinners, catering formats, package inclusions, venue requirements and next steps.
- Replaced internal website/SEO language and repetitive slogans with customer-facing explanations.
- Rewrote all 15 live comparison articles as transparent provider-published buying guides. Removed unsupported winner/ranking claims and unverified competitor prices. Their stored content blocks were updated too, so a later import cannot restore the superseded claims.
- Reworked 16 planning guides with practical sections, sources/further reading, sensible service comparisons and current metadata. Food-policy articles direct readers to the current authority and setting-specific requirements instead of asserting unsupported universal rules.
- Reconciled membership fee/credit arithmetic, yacht examples, the privacy contact number and the six-course chef sample menu.
- Distinguished first replies from completed proposals across enquiries, FAQs and the thank-you page.
- Removed duplicated analytics setup and WhatsApp handling from the HTML shell.
- Preserved calculator format, staffing and guest choices through WhatsApp, form and email enquiry preparation.
- Reconciled the existing yacht redirects across routes, navigation, SEO records, internal links and sitemap. Historical source pages are removed from the SPA; the already-existing Vercel redirects remain.
- Removed remaining inbound links to intentionally parked pages while preserving the pages and service-area information.
- Included images, FAQs, dates and references in article hydration data so the first interactive render matches the production HTML.
- Made sitemap regeneration preserve existing modification dates, with authored article dates taking precedence.

## Search strategy

The SEO contract retains one primary owner for each active target. Fifteen existing comparison articles have now been registered, including four relevant secondary topics each. Those new secondary topics are editorial mappings, not measured demand. No keyword search volumes, traffic improvements or ranking gains are invented.

Keep headings readable. Primary phrases belong where they naturally describe the service; secondary phrases should support the body. Do not create a new URL solely because a phrase can be listed. Parked pages, household support pages and noindex topic hubs remain excluded from the sitemap. The household chef-roster page keeps its existing cross-canonical decision; changing that is a separate strategy decision.

## What still needs business evidence

The repository cannot prove chef certifications, partner licences, insurance cover, operational capacity, offer availability, membership fulfilment or social-account ownership. Existing customer testimonials have not been independently authenticated. The finding register distinguishes these from defects that can be corrected in code.

Privacy wording now reflects visible tracking integrations and the correct contact details, but this work does not establish the legal entity, vendor agreements, retention periods or consent requirements. Those depend on actual business operations. No payment, booking or production email was submitted during testing.

The footer’s breadth, enquiry overlays and conversion design are documented as future UX work. This update focuses on content quality, SEO consistency and observed functional defects; it does not claim that all possible design, security, legal or operational issues have been eliminated.
