## Page analysis (done before any prompt)

This is not a food-porn article. It is a **hiring guide**. The reader is an affluent host who wants to know: what it costs, how booking works, whether the chef is vetted, and whether they can stay a guest in their own home.

Commercial job of the images:

1. **Hero** — prove the outcome: you remain at the table; a full team runs the night in a real luxury villa.
2. **Frame 2** — prove the process: skilled people cooking in *your* kitchen, not a restaurant pass.
3. **Frame 3** — prove relief: the host is seated, the team is finishing, the house still looks like a home.

What this page is **not**:

- A religious gathering
- An office lunch
- A plated-food hero with no people
- A handshake / contract / laptop scene

### Continuity sheet

- **Home:** Lived-in Palm / Emirates Hills style villa. Open kitchen to dining. Warm stone, timber, linen. Pool glow through glass at night. Books, art, not a showroom.
- **Host:** Woman, late 30s, Northern European / international, dark-blonde hair, silk blouse, quiet gold earrings. She is the person who hired myCHEF.
- **Guests:** Four affluent international friends, 35–50. Contemporary luxury clothes. Mixed European and East Asian faces at the table is fine. Not a ceremonial group.
- **Head chef:** Man, early 40s, Southern European or Levantine kitchen background, clean whites, calm, plating — not posing.
- **Assistant:** Filipina, early 30s, dark service kit, resetting a glass or checking a tray. Dignified, skilled.
- **Time:** Blue hour into candlelight. Same evening across all three frames.
- **Meal state:** Hero = last plate going down. Frame 2 = active prep, 90 minutes earlier. Frame 3 = mid-meal, candles lower.
