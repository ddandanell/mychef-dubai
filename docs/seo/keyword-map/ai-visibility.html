Here's a breakdown of what private chefs in Dubai typically cost, and which providers actually publish their pricing.

## Per-Dinner Pricing

Prices vary a lot depending on the group size, menu complexity, and the chef's caliber:

- **Take a Chef** (takeachef.com/en-ae/private-chef/dubai) publishes clear per-head tiers: 
booking prices range from 305 AED to 482 AED per person, with rates of 305 AED for 13+ people, 314 AED for 7-12 people, and higher for smaller groups.


- **Chef On Demand** (chefondemand.it) is one of the most transparent, publishing detailed tiered menus: 
around AED 408 per person for an Essential dinner (4 courses, 6 guests), dropping to about AED 351 per head at 8 guests; a Gourmet menu (5 courses) runs around AED 477 per person at 6 guests, and a Luxury menu (6+ courses) about AED 627 per person at the same group size.
 They also note 
a four-guest dinner runs higher per head, and premium tasting menus can pass AED 1,000 per person.
 Surcharges apply too — 
Christian and public-holiday surcharges add 50% in Dubai, and Ramadan iftar or New Year's Eve bookings are custom-quoted, never a flat rate.


- **Tip a Chef** (tipachef.com/private-chef/dubai) gives a broader event-based range: 
for a private dinner event, expect AED 1,500–AED 5,000 depending on guest count and menu complexity.


- **MiumMium** (miummium.com/en-ae/personal-chef-dubai) quotes in USD: 
the average sum paid over the last few years for their Personal Chef services was $50.65 + tip per person, with the lowest priced meals in the low thirties and the highest over $150 per guest.


- General market estimates (e.g., from Solai and Alliance Recruitment) suggest a wider spread: 
for smaller gatherings or simple meals, a private chef in Dubai may charge starting from around 300 to 600 AED per person, while a more refined multi-course experience typically ranges from 600 to 1,200 AED per person.
 At the top end, 
high-end private chefs, especially those with fine dining backgrounds or international experience, may charge 1,200 to 3,000 AED or more per person.


## Monthly / Ongoing Pricing

- **Tip a Chef** publishes ongoing service rates: 
meal prep services run AED 900–AED 2,000 per day, while a full-time private chef in Dubai earns AED 180,000–AED 380,000 per year
 (roughly AED 15,000–31,600/month as a salaried live-in or full-time hire).

- **Alliance Recruitment Agency** (alliancerecruitmentagency.ae) gives a lower live-in salary estimate: 
for a chef who lives with the family as a housing member, salaries may vary from AED 3,000 to AED 3,500 per month
 — this figure likely reflects a live-in domestic staff arrangement rather than a professional trained chef, so treat it as a low-end benchmark. They also separately note 
an average price for a private chef may cost AED 400 to AED 500 per person
 for one-off events.
