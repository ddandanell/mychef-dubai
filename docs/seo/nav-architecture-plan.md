# Nav architecture plan — one spine, one primary, 12–16 sections

**Site:** mychef.ae  
**Scope:** every URL in the header mega-menus  
**Law:** `skills/mychef-writing-system/` + `docs/seo/myCHEF-AE-SEO-STANDARD.json` `pages[path]`.  
The 500-keyword architecture file and `mychef-master-keywords.json` do not get a vote.

This is the plan. It is not a rewrite of 50 pages in one pass.

---

## 0. What is true before any page is touched

1. **One primary per URL.** Title, H1, first 100 words, one subheading. Subs in sentences. Cap eight.
2. **If this page does not own a term, link to the page that does.** Never compete.
3. **Structure follows the questions the customer is asking at this URL** — not a template of six icon cards.
4. **12–16 sections** on every commercial page. Hubs route. Spokes go deep on *their* constraint. Contact/trust pages use a shorter spine (8–12).
5. **Price method before or with the number.** Published bands live on `/dubai-catering-prices-guide` and `/private-chef-dubai/pricing`. Occasion pages quote those bands and link. They do not invent a new rate card.
6. **No brochure words.** Elegant, seamless, unforgettable, world-class, photograph-worthy — out.
7. **Volumes are mostly unknown.** The master map says no keyword tool returned UAE volume. Do not invent ranks. Prioritise by nav position, commercial intent, and whether the live page is still a clone.

## 1. The universal spine (14, flex 12–16)

Read only the H2s. If they do not tell the story of *this* URL, the page is not done.

| # | Job | Typical label | Always? |
|---|-----|---------------|---------|
| 1 | What this is | Hero — primary in H1 | Yes |
| 2 | Early proof | Trust strip | Yes |
| 3 | Mechanism | WHAT THIS IS | Yes |
| 4 | Who / who not | WHO IT IS FOR | Yes — this is where cannibalisation is killed |
| 5 | How the thing is used | HOW GUESTS EAT / HOW THE HOUSE RUNS / HOW THE DAY RUNS | Yes |
| 6 | Scope | INCLUDED | Yes — always / when agreed / not included |
| 7 | Money | COST | Yes on commercial pages; hub may point only |
| 8 | Constraint of *this* page | WHERE / THE KITCHEN / THE VESSEL / THE OFFICE / THE CUISINE | **The unique section** |
| 9 | Menu or matching | THE MENU / THE CHEF | If this page owns it; else one paragraph + link |
| 10 | Diet | DIETARY | Occasion + format + cuisine; skip on Contact |
| 11 | People on the night | STAFF / TOAST / SERVICE | Catering and events; not household chef |
| 12 | Sequence | HOW IT WORKS | Numbered. Do not duplicate How It Works cluster copy. |
| 13 | Proof visual | GALLERY or case | 4 frames max; “Experience concept shown” |
| 14 | Residual + place + act | FAQ · locations · CTA | FAQ only what the page has not taught |

**To hit 16:** split (4) into for / not; split (9) into menu + packages; add a sibling-cluster rail.  
**To stay at 12:** merge 10+11, merge 13 into 3, keep FAQ short.

**What never becomes a section:** “Why choose us.” “Our process.” “Our chefs” on a page that does not own chefs. A six-card icon grid of synonyms.

## 2. Four templates (do not paste blindly)

### A — Hub (nav root)

Job: define the product, split the traffic, send people to the spoke that owns the next question.

Sections: 1–4, **spoke index** (replaces 5), 6–7 (short), 8 = “where this sits vs the other three products”, 12, 14. Gallery optional. **14 sections if the hub currently dumps every format on itself — it must not.**

### B — Format spoke (Catering mega)

Job: one way of serving food. The unique section is **operational constraint** (heat holding, one-hand eating, wind on a grill, no kitchen at drop-off).

Must link up to `/catering-dubai` and sideways to 2–3 formats that are often combined (canapés → plated; buffet → live stations).

Must link out to occasions (wedding, corporate, birthday) with *their* anchors — never “buffet catering dubai” on the wedding page as if wedding owned it.

### C — Occasion spoke (Events mega)

Job: one night, one fear (the room, the guests, the clock). Unique section is **who is in the room** (two families, children, the speech, the award).

Must link: format owners, cuisine owners, `/catering-dubai`, adjacent occasion (engagement → wedding; birthday → kids).

### D — Cuisine spoke

Job: one kitchen identity. Unique section is **how this cuisine is served in Dubai catering** (sharing vs plated, live bread, dietary defaults).

Must not become a fake occasion page. Link occasions as “this cuisine at a wedding / iftar / office.”

### E — Experience spoke (Dinner mega)

Job: the experience *is* the product. Unique section is **the moment** (two people, desert logistics, class vs dinner). Do not sell household chef backup language here.

### F — Cluster support (Private Chef children)

Already designed. Do not apply the catering 14 blindly. How It Works / Our Chefs / Quality / Privacy / Pricing each own one argument. Cross-link; do not restated the hub.

### G — Contact / trust

Not a sales landing. 8–12 sections. No keyword war with commercial hubs.

## 14. What “done” looks like on any one URL

- Searcher who typed the primary feels home after the H1.
- 12–16 sections, H2s readable as a story.
- Price method present or linked, never a surprise.
- At least 6 internal links to **owners**, none targeting a sibling primary.
- FAQ ≤ 10, residual only.
- Gallery is of *this* occasion/format, labelled Experience concept shown.
- Final-editor score ≥ 16 before it ships.
