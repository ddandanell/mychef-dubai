## Customer experience

- The page explains qualification, a detailed brief, activation, availability checks, introductions, a paid home trial, the 30-day Learning Month and monthly relationship management.

- The rematching explanation covers no new activation for substantially the same role during an active agreement, while making trial fees, possible new chef costs, temporary cover and availability clear.
- Optional teaching and family recipe recording are subject to the chef's capability and the agreed scope.
- Three original editorial images illustrate household dining, the personal brief and family recipe learning. They are not named staff portraits or client case studies. Exact prompts and responsive asset paths are in `managed-household-images.json`.
